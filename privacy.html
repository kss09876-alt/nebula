Draft · 2026-11-12

This is a draft. It must be updated to match the services actually used once sign-in and payments are added, and reviewed by a legal professional.

1. What we collect today

NEBULA currently has no sign-in or payments, and we do not collect or store personal data on our servers. Your voyager name, likes, viewing history and language/sound settings are stored only on your device.

2. What we will collect once paid service begins

  • Account: the identifier, email and (optionally) name passed on by your sign-in provider (Apple, Google, Kakao)
  • Purchase records: product, time of purchase, the store’s or processor’s transaction ID, subscription expiry (we never receive or store card numbers or other payment-method details)
  • Support: the email address and content you send us

3. Why

To identify you, provide the exhibitions you bought, manage subscriptions, handle refunds and enquiries, and meet legal obligations.

4. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account; payment and contract records are kept for 5 years as required by Korean e-commerce law.
